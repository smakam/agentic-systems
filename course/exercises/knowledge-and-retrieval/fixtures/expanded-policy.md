# Lab extended seller payout policy

This document is a synthetic teaching fixture. It does not change real marketplace rules or records. Its purpose is to test retrieval of a rule and its adjacent exception.

Sellers register their business name and support contact before listing products. A seller must maintain an accurate contact address for notices about payments and returns.

Listings include a product description, price, stock count, and seller reference. Updating a listing does not retrospectively alter the price of an already captured purchase.

Payment capture records the amount received for a purchase. Authorization without capture is insufficient evidence of settled payment. A support explanation must distinguish these states.

Shipment records establish whether delivery occurred. A label being created, a package being dispatched, and delivery being confirmed are different events. Eligibility uses confirmed delivery time.

Under the standard seller payout rule, release eligibility requires captured payment, confirmed delivery, no open return, and at least 24 elapsed hours after delivery. This standard rule is subject to the immediately following exception.

Exception to the standard seller payout rule: a purchase carrying the lab-only Amber Review flag requires 72 elapsed hours after confirmed delivery, rather than 24. Captured payment and no open return are still required. This flag changes eligibility only; it does not prove release was executed.

An open return blocks eligibility while it remains unresolved. Closing a return does not by itself prove a refund occurred. Completed refund records establish the actual monetary adjustment.

Failed refunds do not establish a successful return of funds. Support must identify the recorded refund status rather than treating every refund request as completed.

Seller balances describe captured sales, refunded amounts, marketplace fees, paid payouts, and unpaid amounts. An unpaid amount may still be held and is not automatically available for immediate release.

Support explanations should cite the retrieved policy version and identify the business facts used. Policy describes conditions; database evidence establishes which actions occurred.

Operators may review an eligible payout but a policy passage does not execute the payout. A completed action needs a corresponding recorded status and event.

When evidence is missing, state which facts need verification. Avoid inventing a payment, delivery time, return status, or payout execution event merely to produce a definitive answer.
