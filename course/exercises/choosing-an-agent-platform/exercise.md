# Choosing an Agent Platform — learner exercise

## Workspace and goal

Use the chapter flowchart, your browser and the [two company requirements briefs](requirements.md). No coding, deployment, new account or paid model call is required. Keep the [reference answers](solutions/answers.md) closed until you finish.

The lesson demonstrates three worked examples. Now apply the same method to **Company D and Company E**, which have different requirements. Prepare about one page per company; compact tables are sufficient.

## Questions to answer for each company

1. **Requirements:** What is the task? Which requirements are hard constraints, and which are preferences? What important information is missing?
2. **Shortlist:** Name two plausible products and their deployment paths. Which flowchart branches led you to them? Explain why each belongs on the shortlist.
3. **Ownership:** What does each platform supply, what must the team configure/integrate, and what must it build or operate? Include the agent/framework, runtime, business tools, state and operational controls.
4. **Costs and control:** Compare models, infrastructure, core agent platform, supporting services and team effort. Count bundled services once; place licenses/subscriptions under the service they pay for. Exact rates are optional.
5. **Recommendation:** Which option would you try first, and why? State one requirement change or finding that would reverse your decision. If a hard constraint remains unverified, make the recommendation conditional.
6. **Validation:** Design a small trial using the same task, facts, permissions and success criteria. What quality, authorization, state, trace, latency and cost evidence would you need? Design the trial; do not execute it.

## Submit and self-check

Submit both decisions: requirements, two-option comparison, recommendation, remaining gates, reversal condition and trial plan. No screenshots of private settings or invented benchmark results are needed.

Can another person explain why your choice fits this company's requirements and operating capacity? Could they identify what your team still owns, and what evidence might change the decision? There is no universal winning platform.
