# Seller settlement

Captured buyer funds are allocated to each purchased item. Marketplace fee is 10 percent, rounded down in paise. The seller payable is gross less completed refunds and the adjusted fee. All transactions are simulated and denominated in INR.

Payout requires delivery confirmation and a 24-hour hold after delivery. Requested, approved or received returns block release. A payout failure leaves funds unpaid; retry is a separate operator action. Delivery alone does not imply a completed payout.

Completed refunds reverse the fee proportionally using 10 percent of the remaining gross, rounded down. Post-payout refunds need manual review in this demo; automatic recovery is unsupported.
